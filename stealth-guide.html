STEALTH GUIDE

Master the art of silent elimination

🎯 STEALTH BASICS

👁️

Detection Awareness

Watch enemy vision cones. Yellow means caution, red means detected.

🤫

Sound Matters

Running creates noise. Walk to stay hidden. Use distractions.

🌙

Shadow Bonus

Attack from darkness for bonus damage and instant stealth kill.

🗡️ STEALTH KILLS

Silent Takedown

  • • Approach from behind
  • • Press attack when close
  • • Enemy goes down silently
  • • Works on unaware enemies

Shadow Strike

  • • Attack from darkness
  • • Deals 3x damage
  • • Instant kill possible
  • • No alert triggered

Aerial Takedown

  • • Drop from above
  • • Heavy damage on impact
  • • Silent if timed right
  • • Use ledges and roofs

Corner Ambush

  • • Wait at corners
  • • Guard walks past
  • • Quick strike and hide
  • • Perfect for checkpoints

👻 GHOST ROUTE TIPS

Key Principles

  • • No Kills - Never kill enemies
  • • No Detection - Stay hidden at all times
  • • No Alerts - Don't trigger alarm states

Techniques

  • • Knock out instead of kill
  • • Use smoke bombs for escape
  • • Employ decoys and distractions
  • • Master the crouch-walk

💡 ADVANCED TIPS

Patrol Timing

Study guard routes. Time your movements between patrol intervals.

Body Hide

Move knocked out enemies to hiding spots. Other guards won't find them.

Multiple Enemies

Use chained knockouts. One guard distracts, you take down the others.

Save Often

Create manual saves before risky sections. Ghost runs require patience.
