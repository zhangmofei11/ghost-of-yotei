Characters

Meet the characters of Ghost of Yotei.

Playable Character

🥷

The Ronin (Protagonist)

A wandering warrior with a mysterious past. Master of both sword and stealth.

Role

Ronin / Ghost

Combat Style

Katana, Bow, Stealth

Special Ability

Ghost Stance

Allies

Lady Ayaka

A noblewoman who joins your cause. Provides intelligence and support.

  • • Location: Chapter 2
  • • Role: Quest giver, story character
  • • Combat: Support, not direct

Master Tanaka

A retired samurai who trains you in advanced techniques.

  • • Location: Chapter 3
  • • Role: Combat trainer
  • • Unlocks: Ghost stance training

Kenji

A young villager who becomes your apprentice.

  • • Location: Chapter 2
  • • Role: Companion missions
  • • Special: Can learn stealth from you

The Blacksmith

An eccentric smith who upgrades your equipment.

  • • Location: Town hub
  • • Role: Equipment upgrades
  • • Special: Legendary weapon crafting

Enemies

Lord Oda

The main antagonist. A ruthless warlord seeking conquest.

  • • Location: Final boss
  • • Difficulty: Extreme
  • • Weakness: Requires Ghost stance

General Raiden

Oda's right-hand man. Elite warrior and strategist.

  • • Location: Chapter 4
  • • Difficulty: Hard
  • • Weakness: Exploit opening after combos

The Shadow Clan

An assassination guild working for Oda.

  • • Location: Various chapters
  • • Difficulty: Medium-Hard
  • • Special: Stealth specialists

Mongrel Dogs

Oda's personal guard. Highly disciplined soldiers.

  • • Location: Throughout game
  • • Difficulty: Easy-Medium
  • • Count: Multiple encounters
