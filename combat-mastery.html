⚔️ COMBAT STANCES

🗡️

Stone Stance

Defensive, counter attacks

  • • Strong against rushes
  • • Perfect parry window
  • • Counter damage bonus
🌊

Flowing Stance

Balanced, multi-target

  • • Wide sweeping attacks
  • • Good vs groups
  • • Medium parry window
🔥

Moon Stance

Aggressive, high damage

  • • Fast attack chain
  • • High risk/reward
  • • Narrow parry window

🗡️ SWORD TECHNIQUES

Light Attack Chain

Square → Square → Square

  • • Fast 3-hit combo
  • • Low damage per hit
  • • Good for building resolve

Heavy Attack

Triangle (Hold for charged)

  • • High damage output
  • • Stuns enemies
  • • Breaks guards

Perfect Parry

Block just before hit lands

  • • 2x counter damage
  • • Stuns attacker
  • • Builds ultimate gauge

Resolve Attack

L1+Triangle when resolve full

  • • Massive damage
  • • Ignores defense
  • • One-shots most enemies

💡 COMBAT TIPS

Enemy Reading

Watch enemy stance. Red glow means attack incoming.

Stance Switching

Change stances based on enemy type. Stone vs bosses, Moon vs weak.

Environment

Use ledges for aerial attacks. Push enemies off cliffs.

Phase 2: Execute
